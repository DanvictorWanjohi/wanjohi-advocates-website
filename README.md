# Wanjohi Advocates Website Prompt

Use this prompt to plan and create a polished, professional website for Wanjohi Advocates, a law firm based in Nairobi, Kenya.

## Objective

Explain the firm’s services clearly and encourage relevant inquiries and consultation requests from individuals, businesses, companies and institutions.

## Firm details

- **Firm name:** Wanjohi Advocates
- **Tagline:** Where Law Meets Integrity
- **Location:** Monrovia Street, Nairobi, Kenya
- **Phone:** 0758 242 385
- **Email:** wanjohiadvocates@gmail.com
- **Brand direction:** Light blue with a law-related logo
- **Primary audience:** Individuals, businesses, companies and institutions
- **Main objective:** Generate relevant inquiries and consultation requests

## Accuracy requirements

Before writing copy or making legal claims, ask for any missing firm details that would materially affect the website. Use only information supplied or verified by the firm. Do not invent credentials, years of experience, case outcomes, client names, awards, fees or guarantees. Do not present website content as legal advice. Mark missing information with clear placeholders and flag it for verification.

## Website structure

1. **Home:** Write a clear headline explaining who the firm helps and how. Include a concise introduction, a prominent “Request a Consultation” call to action, an overview of confirmed practice areas, verified trust signals, and the Nairobi location and contact details.
2. **About:** Include the firm story, lawyers’ profiles, qualifications and professional memberships only after the firm confirms them.
3. **Services:** Create a dedicated section or page for each confirmed practice area. Explain the types of matters handled, who the service is for and how a prospective client can take the next step. Avoid promising outcomes.
4. **Insights:** Provide space for articles or updates with author and publication date. Do not fabricate legal developments or cite outdated law as current.
5. **Contact:** Include phone, email, office location, confirmed business hours and a short inquiry form. Tell visitors not to submit confidential or urgent information through the form.
6. **Footer:** Include navigation, contact details, a privacy notice and any firm or professional information the firm confirms should appear.

## Design and user experience

Use a refined, approachable visual identity suited to a Nairobi law firm. Make the site mobile-friendly, accessible, quick to scan and easy to navigate. Use readable typography, strong contrast, clear headings and visible contact buttons. Avoid courthouse clichés and stock imagery that implies specific clients or case results. Ensure the website works well on phones and supports keyboard navigation.

## Copy and conversion

Write in clear, confident, plain English for the firm’s intended clients. Make the firm’s services easy to understand without overstating expertise. Use specific, helpful calls to action such as “Discuss your matter” or “Request a consultation.” Do not use fear-based claims, guarantees or unsupported comparisons.

## Deliverables

Provide:

- A recommended sitemap and page-by-page content outline.
- Draft website copy with placeholders clearly marked where firm information is missing.
- A visual direction describing layout, colors, typography and imagery.
- Suggested inquiry form fields and concise privacy/confidentiality wording.

Before finalizing, check every factual statement against the information provided by the firm and flag anything that needs verification.
